# HeatShift business model

See [Business_Model.pdf](submission/Business_Model.pdf) for the complete proposal.

- **User:** households, students and outdoor planners; currently three airport stations.
- **Possible payer:** universities, event teams and small logistics businesses. These are hypotheses, not customers.
- **Value:** a temperature estimate, visible uncertainty, data-quality decisions and cited evidence in one dashboard.
- **Revenue hypothesis:** a basic public view plus institutional reporting/support subscription. PKR 15,000/month is only an example price to test.
- **Prototype cost:** existing free static Space and public Actions workflow; no paid plan purchased. Provider quotas/terms apply.
- **Commercial costs:** appropriate Open-Meteo previous-runs license, hosting/storage, maintenance/support. Obtain real quotes.
- **Usage estimate:** 96 upstream requests/day at hourly frequency before retries/manual use.
- **Break-even:** fixed monthly cost / (price minus variable cost per customer). No profitability claim without actual costs.
- **Impact:** planning clarity and data literacy are potential benefits. Measure completion, comprehension and repeat use in a pilot; no measured loss avoidance or safety impact.

Commercial API permissions must be checked against [Open-Meteo terms/plans](https://open-meteo.com/en/pricing). Free prototype access is not commercial permission.
